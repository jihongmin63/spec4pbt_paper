\section{A SpecTec-Based Toolchain}
\label{sec:instantiation}

\subsection{Specification and Property Inputs}
\label{sec:specification-inputs}

The toolchain accepts a SpecTec specification and a user-supplied property in a
restricted SpecTec-like notation with explicit inputs, premises, and a conclusion.
It translates the specification into Lean and derives a Lean proposition,
generator proof obligations, and an executable checker from the property.
The generator targets the property's precondition, while the checker evaluates
its conclusion.

\subsection{Generator Construction and Verification}
\label{sec:generator-verification}

An agent constructs a generator for the property's precondition together with
soundness and completeness proofs, which Lean checks.
Soundness requires every possible output of the generator to satisfy the
precondition;
completeness requires every input within the declared domain that satisfies the
precondition to be a possible output.
These guarantees concern the generator's possible outputs, rather than their
sampling frequencies.

\subsection{Testing Language Implementations}
\label{sec:implementation-testing}

The user identifies specification relations whose behavior is externally
observable and supplies adapters that invoke the implementation and map its
observed outcomes to the relations' results.
The toolchain binds the required relations to these adapters and evaluates the
property's conclusion on generated inputs using the observed outcomes.
The soundness and completeness proofs establish the generator's correctness with
respect to the Lean definitions;
the correspondence of the SpecTec translation, executable checker, and external
adapters must be established separately.

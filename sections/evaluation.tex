\subsection{Validating the SpecTec-to-Rocq Translation}
\label{sec:translation-correctness}

% TODO: 증명 방법(실행 함수 + Eval 건전성 정리 + vm_compute 등), 증명 작성 주체, SL/AL 차이 사례의 분류 기준 미정
The guarantees of Section~\ref{sec:guarantee} hold for the Rocq predicates, so
they carry over to the SpecTec property only if the translation preserves the
meaning of the specification.
We validate the translation of the relations that the preconditions use, such
as \texttt{Program\_ok}, on the test programs of p4c, the P4 reference compiler.
For each program, the P4-SpecTec interpreter yields success with an output,
normal failure, or abort, and this result is stated as a proposition about the
Rocq relation and proven in Rocq.
Agreement on these programs supports the translation but does not prove it
correct for all programs.

\subsection{Generalization Beyond P4}
\label{sec:beyond}

Although our evaluation focuses on P4, the approach can also be applied to other languages given their SpecTec specifications.
SpecTec can express other languages such as WebAssembly~\cite{youn24spectec} and even algorithms outside programming languages, such as Ethereum consensus~\cite{jeong26spectrum}.
Beyond SpecTec, the approach may apply to other specification languages if the three challenges in Section~\ref{sec:three-propositions} can be addressed algorithmically.
